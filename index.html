About Me

Designing aerospace systems with purpose.

I am currently completing a Master of Engineering in Aerospace Engineering at RMIT University in Melbourne.

My academic and project experience includes aircraft design, aerodynamic analysis, UAV-system development, propulsion selection, stability and control, computational analysis and engineering research.

I enjoy transforming mission requirements into practical engineering decisions. My work combines theoretical analysis with tools such as MATLAB, Python, XFLR5, ANSYS, SolidWorks and CATIA.

Career Objective

To contribute to an aerospace organisation where I can apply engineering analysis, practical design skills and research experience to meaningful aviation projects.
