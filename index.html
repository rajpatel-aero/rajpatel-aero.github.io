About Me

Engineering ideas into practical aerospace solutions.

I am currently pursuing a Master of Engineering in Aerospace at RMIT University in Melbourne. I hold a Bachelor of Technology in Aeronautical Engineering and have developed experience across aircraft design, UAV systems, propulsion, aerodynamics, simulation and engineering research.

My experience includes aerospace and robotic-system design, airframe optimisation, technical calculations, prototype support, AI-based aircraft fault diagnostics and experimental aerodynamic research.

I am particularly interested in multidisciplinary aircraft design, high-performance UAVs, flight mechanics, propulsion integration and data-driven aerospace engineering.
